## [1.0.0] - 27 November 2026
### Added
- Canadian address settings for the Loki Checkout: "Address" and "Apartment, suite, etc (optional)" street labels, "Province" region label, and a "Postal code" field with A1A 1A1 placeholder and input mask
- Full-width second street line for Canadian addresses in the default skin
- Separate layout handles for Canadian shipping and billing addresses, so each address form is configured independently
